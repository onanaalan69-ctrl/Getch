GETCH
===============================================================================================

What if you could play a platformer that is in *continuous development*? See my progress through the development of this game, and enjoy the old, good, and simple hops of 2D platformer games :)

> ## Link to [play](https://onanaalan69-ctrl.itch.io/getch) the game!

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to launch the game?
===============================================================================================

You click on the link above! It will automatically open in your browser

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Features
================================================================================================
+ A main menu scene with background music and level selection
+ Each level has at least 20 collectable items (represented by cherries and bananas)
+ A freshly designed tile map (not pre-prepared or copied)
+ Double jump and wall jump mechanics
+ Level switching mechanics at the end of each level
+ The game is *Dynamically developed* (still in development, so the feature section is subject to change)
+ And of course *more than 50 platforms* just waiting for the next hop!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Quick Start
=============================================================================================
The game has three main commands: UP, LEFT, and RIGHT. These commands are controlled with the directional keys, or the A key for left, D for right, and SPACE for jump.

The double jump cannot be activated more than once in a single jump, and wall jump is achieved by pressing a jump key when in contact with the *wall only*
